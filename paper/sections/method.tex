\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Preliminaries: ODE Initialization in Self Forcing}
\label{sec:method:ode}

Let $\Gphi$ be the few-step autoregressive student. For frame $i$, the student
takes the noisy frame $\xt$, the clean context frames $\xctx$, and the
timestep $t$. The target $y$ is the clean frame from the teacher ODE
trajectory. The standard ODE initialization loss is
\begin{equation}
  \LODE = \E \big\| \Gphi(\xt, \xctx, t) - y \big\|^2 .
  \label{eq:ode}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Swift ODE Initialization}
\label{sec:method:swift}

Let $\Gweak$ be a weak model. We use an early checkpoint of the few-step
student. We move the regression target away from the prediction of the weak
model:
\begin{equation}
  \LSwift = \E \Big\| \Gphi(\xt, \xctx, t)
    - \Big[ y + \alpha \big( y - \Gweak(\xt, \xctx, t) \big) \Big] \Big\|^2 ,
  \label{eq:swift}
\end{equation}
where $\alpha \ge 0$ controls the strength of the correction. When
$\alpha = 0$, \cref{eq:swift} is equal to \cref{eq:ode}.
